Here is an uncomfortable truth for any UK service business that has spent money on SEO: ranking number one on Google no longer guarantees you get seen. You can be top of the page and still invisible to a growing share of searchers. Schema markup: why your website needs it

Your website looks great to you. To Google and the AI tools your clients now ask for recommendations, a lot of it is invisible. That is what schema markup fixes. It is a small piece of code in the background that spells out the facts a search engine cannot reliably guess: what your firm does, the areas you cover, your opening hours and your reviews. No client ever sees it, but it is the difference between a tool confidently saying this practice covers your area and opens at nine, and skipping you because it was not sure. Three to get right first: LocalBusiness, so your service, area and hours are clear; Service, so each thing you offer is spelled out; and Review, so your ratings show up where they count. You do not need to code it by hand. Most website builders have a plugin, or a developer can add it in an afternoon. It is one of the cheapest ways for a UK service business to get found by the tools clients actually use. Full guide on our blog.
